# Animal Food Preference

Guides animal food selection to prioritize renewable and cheap food sources first, preserving your valuable meals, pemmican, and survival rations.

## Features

- Configurable tier-based food priority system for animals
- Auto-classifies all food items (including modded) into categories
- Per-item overrides via Mod Settings for fine-grained control
- Distance preference configuration

## How It Works

Animals in RimWorld choose food based on an "optimality" score. This mod adds a large offset to that score based on the food's category, so animals strongly prefer cheap/renewable food over valuable meals.

The default priority order (most preferred first):

1. Wild grass, bushes, dandelions
2. Flowers and decorative crops
3. Corpses
4. Hay
5. Kibble
6. Nutrient paste meals
7. Simple meals
8. Fine meals
9. Lavish meals
10. Raw vegetables and meat
11. Pemmican
12. Insect jelly
13. Survival meals
14. Food crops (harvestable plants)

All priorities are fully configurable in Mod Settings.
